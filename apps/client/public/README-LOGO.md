# 🎨 Add Your Logo Here

## Quick Start

**1. Place your logo in this folder:**
```
apps/client/public/logo.png
```

**2. That's it!** Your logo will automatically appear in:
- ✅ Dashboard sidebar
- ✅ Mobile header
- ✅ Login page
- ✅ Register page

---

## Logo Requirements

✅ **Format**: PNG with transparent background  
✅ **Size**: 200×200px to 512×512px  
✅ **File name**: Must be exactly `logo.png` (lowercase)  
✅ **File size**: Under 100KB (optimized)

## Quick Tips

💡 **Use transparent background** for best results  
💡 **Optimize file size** with TinyPNG or similar  
💡 **Test on mobile** and desktop views  
💡 **Clear browser cache** if logo doesn't update (Ctrl+Shift+R)  

---

**Logo component automatically handles**:
- ✅ Different screen sizes (responsive)
- ✅ Light and dark backgrounds
- ✅ Loading errors (graceful fallback)
- ✅ Caching for fast loading
